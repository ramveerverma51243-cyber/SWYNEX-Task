# SWYNEX Task 2 – Exploratory Data Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on performing Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the cleaned Global E-Commerce Sales dataset prepared in Task 1.

The analysis was performed using SQL and Tableau Public to identify sales patterns, category performance, country-wise sales, and monthly sales trends.

## Dataset

The dataset contains 1,200 e-commerce sales records with the following columns:

- Order_ID
- Country
- Category
- Unit_Price
- Quantity
- Order_Date
- Total_Amount

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Total Sales

Total sales generated from the dataset:

**₹1,512,252.98**

### 2. Total Quantity Sold

Total quantity sold:

**6,008 units**

### 3. Country-wise Sales

Sales were analyzed across different countries to compare their contribution to total sales.

The highest sales were recorded from:

- USA – ₹206,117.53
- UAE – ₹185,632.17
- Germany – ₹165,844.33

### 4. Category-wise Sales

Sales were analyzed by product category.

Top 3 categories by sales:

- Home Decor – ₹232,275.86
- Grocery – ₹201,355.28
- Electronics – ₹199,641.80

### 5. Category-wise Quantity

Total quantity sold was analyzed for each product category to understand product demand.

### 6. Monthly Sales Trend

Monthly sales were analyzed from January 2025 to November 2025.

The highest monthly sales were recorded in:

**July 2025 – ₹180,208.16**

November 2025 recorded the lowest sales:

**₹12,285.24**

No December records were present in the dataset.

## Key Insights

1. Home Decor generated the highest category-wise sales at ₹232,275.86.
2. Grocery was the second-highest category by sales at ₹201,355.28.
3. Electronics ranked third in category-wise sales at ₹199,641.80.
4. July 2025 recorded the highest monthly sales at ₹180,208.16.
5. November 2025 showed a significant decrease in sales compared with the other months.

## Visualization

The analysis was visualized using Tableau Public.

The dashboard includes:

- Country-wise Sales
- Category-wise Sales
- Monthly Sales Trend

The exploratory analysis helped identify important sales patterns across countries, product categories, and months. The Tableau dashboard provides a visual summary of the key findings from the dataset.
